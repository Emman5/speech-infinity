Treatment Areas

Where We Can Help

Therapy always begins with a conversation and an evaluation — never a template

Comprehensive Evaluations

Full speech and language assessment with a clear written report and practical recommendations you can act on.

Articulation & Speech Sounds

Targeted therapy for sound errors so your child can be understood at school, at home, and with friends.

Receptive & Expressive Language

Building vocabulary, sentence structure, following directions, and the ability to say what they mean.

Fluency & Stuttering

Strategies that reduce struggle and build easy, confident speech — with the whole family on the same page.

Social & Pragmatic Language

Conversation skills, perspective-taking, and the unwritten rules that make friendships work.

Parent Consultation & Home Strategies

You are with your child every day. We make sure you have the tools to keep progress going between sessions.

Our Approach

Bilingual, Evidence-Based, and Family-Centered
