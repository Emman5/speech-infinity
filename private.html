Treatment Areas

Where We Can Help

Comprehensive Evaluations

Full speech and language assessment with a clear written report and practical recommendations you can act on.

Articulation & Speech Sounds

Our Approach

Bilingual, Evidence-Based, and Family-Centered

Every child communicates differently. We provide individualized speech and language services that respect your child’s language, culture, strengths, and unique communication needs. Families receive knowledgeable, compassionate care grounded in evidence-based practice.

  • Bilingual English & Spanish servicesEvaluations and therapy provided in English and Spanish, with careful consideration of bilingual language development so that language differences are not mistaken for communication disorders.
  • Therapy where communication happensIn-home and community-based services allow children to practice meaningful communication skills in familiar, everyday environments.
  • Individualized, evidence-based careTherapy is thoughtfully designed around your child’s specific needs, strengths, and goals — not a one-size-fits-all approach.
  • Parents are part of the teamWe keep you informed about your child’s goals and progress while providing practical strategies you can use at home to support communication between sessions.

Our goal goes beyond improving speech and language skills — we help children communicate with greater confidence and help families feel supported every step of the way.
