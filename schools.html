For School Districts

Your Trusted Partner in School-Based Speech Therapy

Experienced clinicians · Seamless collaboration · Student-centered support

Request a Partnership Proposal

Speech Infinity Therapy Group understands the unique demands of school-based services. Our experienced clinicians are knowledgeable in TEA requirements, special education procedures, ARD processes, evaluations, IEP development, and compliant service delivery.

We integrate seamlessly into your campus, collaborate closely with educators and special education teams, and provide dependable speech-language services designed to support both student success and district compliance.

We don’t simply provide therapists — we build lasting partnerships with schools.

20+ Years of School-Based Expertise

Decades of hands-on experience within public and charter school special education programs — bringing a deep understanding of the systems, expectations, and realities of school-based services.

Collaboration at Every Step

We work alongside diagnosticians, school psychologists, teachers, administrators, and special education teams throughout the ARD process to support coordinated, compliant, and student-centered services.

Services

Full-Scope Campus Support

Every service is available in English and Spanish, delivered by ASHA-certified, TDLR-licensed clinicians.

Bilingual Evaluations

Comprehensive speech and language evaluations for culturally and linguistically diverse students, with defensible documentation.

Direct Therapy (Bilingual)

Individual and small-group speech and language therapy on campus, scheduled around instructional time.

IEP Development & Goal Writing

Measurable, standards-aligned goals written to withstand review and to actually move students forward.

Progress Monitoring & Data

Consistent data collection and reporting cycles so progress is documented before anyone asks for it.

TEA Compliance & Documentation

Paperwork, timelines, and records handled to state standards — reducing audit exposure for your campus.

Consultation & Classroom Strategies

Coaching for teachers and paraprofessionals on communication supports that carry over into the classroom.

Why Schools Choose Us

A Partner, Not a Placement Agency

We believe the best student outcomes come from strong partnerships, consistent clinicians, and dependable support. That’s why we intentionally maintain a small, carefully selected team of professionals who understand the unique demands of school-based services.

Your campus benefits from familiar faces, responsive communication, and continuity of care throughout the school year — not a revolving door of providers.

Your students are more than a caseload. Your school is more than a contract. We become part of your team.
