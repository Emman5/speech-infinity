Core Offerings

Full-Scope Campus Support

Every service is available in English and Spanish, delivered by ASHA-certified, TDLR-licensed clinicians.

Bilingual Evaluations

Comprehensive speech and language evaluations for culturally and linguistically diverse students, with defensible documentation.

Direct Therapy (Bilingual)

Individual and small-group speech and language therapy on campus, scheduled around instructional time.

IEP Development & Goal Writing

Measurable, standards-aligned goals written to withstand review and to actually move students forward.

Progress Monitoring & Data

Consistent data collection and reporting cycles so progress is documented before anyone asks for it.

TEA Compliance & Documentation

Paperwork, timelines, and records handled to state standards — reducing audit exposure for your campus.

Consultation & Classroom Strategies

Coaching for teachers and paraprofessionals on communication supports that carry over into the classroom.

Why Schools Choose Us

A Partner, Not a Placement Agency
