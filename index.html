Who We Are

Two Decades of School-Based Expertise

Speech Infinity Therapy Group was founded on a simple belief: every student deserves high-quality speech and language therapy services that support their ability to communicate, learn, and succeed in the classroom.

With over 20 years of school-based speech-language pathology experience, we understand the unique needs of students, educators, and school communities. We partner with schools across the Greater Houston area to provide reliable, culturally responsive, and student-centered services focused on positive educational outcomes.
