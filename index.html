About Us

Two decades of school-based expertise.

Speech Infinity Therapy Group was founded on a simple belief: every student deserves a clinician who understands both their language and their learning environment.

With over 20 years of school-based speech-language pathology experience, we bring dedicated support to campuses across the Greater Houston area — driving positive student outcomes, maintaining TEA compliance, and delivering evaluations and therapy in both English and Spanish.

How We Help

Three pathways, one standard of care.

Whether you lead a campus, parent a child, or practice as a therapist, there is a place for you here.

School-Based Services

Contracted speech-language support for charter schools and districts — evaluations, direct therapy, ARD meetings, IEP goal writing, and campus consultation.

Learn More

Private Therapy

Individual evaluations and speech sound therapy delivered in the home or community, with bilingual English/Spanish services and parent coaching built in.
