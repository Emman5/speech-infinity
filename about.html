About Us

Built on Two Decades of Clinical Excellence

Speech Infinity Therapy Group serves the Greater Houston area with bilingual speech-language pathology for schools and families — grounded in compliance, collaboration, and genuine care for students.

Our Mission & Vision

Why We Do This Work

Our Mission

At Speech Infinity Therapy Group, our mission is to empower every child to reach their infinite potential through high-quality, culturally responsive speech and language services.

We partner with families, educators, and school communities to provide individualized, evidence-based care in English and Spanish. Through collaboration, clinical excellence, and compassionate support, we help children find their voice, communicate with confidence, and develop the skills they need to succeed in school and in life.

Our Vision

Our vision is a future where every child has the opportunity to be heard, understood, and empowered to reach their fullest potential.

We strive to be a trusted leader in bilingual speech-language services by building meaningful partnerships with schools and families, developing exceptional clinicians, and creating lasting opportunities for children to communicate, connect, and thrive.

Our Experience

Two Decades of School-Based Expertise

Speech Infinity Therapy Group was founded on a simple belief: every student deserves high-quality speech and language services that support their ability to communicate, learn, and succeed in the classroom.

With over 20 years of school-based speech-language pathology experience, we understand the unique needs of students, educators, and school communities. We partner with schools across the Greater Houston area to provide reliable, culturally responsive, and student-centered services focused on positive educational outcomes.

From comprehensive bilingual evaluations and individualized therapy to ARD collaboration, documentation, and compliance support, we provide schools with the expertise and consistency they need — all while maintaining alignment with TEA and special education requirements.

More than a service provider, we strive to be a trusted partner to every school we serve.

Owner & Founder | Speech Infinity Therapy Group

Janette Vazquez, M.S., CCC-SLP

Janette Vazquez is a bilingual Speech-Language Pathologist with more than 20 years of experience helping children strengthen their communication skills and succeed in school and everyday life. As the Owner and Founder of Speech Infinity Therapy Group, she combines extensive clinical expertise with a strong understanding of school-based services to provide high-quality, dependable support to students, families, and educational teams.

  • 20+ years of experience
  • Bilingual English & Spanish
  • Schools & private practice
  • TEA, ARD & IEP compliance
  • Culturally & linguistically diverse evaluations

Janette earned her Bachelor of Science in Communication Disorders from the University of Houston and her Master's degree in Speech-Language Pathology from the Universitat de Vic in Spain. She holds the Certificate of Clinical Competence in Speech-Language Pathology (CCC-SLP) from the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA) and is a licensed Speech-Language Pathologist in the State of Texas.

Throughout her career, Janette has worked across a variety of settings, including public and charter schools, clinics, home health, and private practice. Her extensive school-based experience includes conducting comprehensive speech and language evaluations, developing individualized education programs (IEPs), participating in and facilitating ARD meetings, collaborating with multidisciplinary teams, and supporting services aligned with Texas Education Agency (TEA) requirements and special education guidelines.

As a bilingual English-Spanish clinician, Janette has specialized expertise in serving culturally and linguistically diverse students. Janette is fluent in speaking, reading, and writing Spanish and has treated numerous non-English speakers. She understands the importance of distinguishing language differences from communication disorders and providing assessment and intervention that considers each child's linguistic and cultural background.

Her approach is grounded in evidence-based practice, individualized intervention, collaboration, and measurable outcomes. She believes that effective speech-language services extend beyond the therapy room and are most successful when clinicians, educators, and families work together toward meaningful goals.

Through Speech Infinity Therapy Group, Janette is committed to providing professional, ethical, culturally responsive, and student-centered speech and language services that help children communicate with confidence and reach their fullest potential.
