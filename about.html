About Us

Built on two decades of clinical excellence.

Speech Infinity Therapy Group serves the Greater Houston area with bilingual speech-language pathology for schools and families — grounded in compliance, collaboration, and genuine care for students.

Owner & Founder

Janette Vazquez, M.S., CCC-SLP

A highly experienced bilingual speech-language pathologist with over 20 years of experience across public school settings and private practice.

Janette holds bachelor's and master's degrees in speech-language pathology and has collaborated with Bilingual Evaluation Specialists of Texas (B.E.S.T.) for more than fifteen years. Over that time she has worked with a wide range of ages and disabilities, developed deep expertise in evaluating culturally and linguistically diverse students, and built a reputation for compliance work that holds up under review.

She founded Speech Infinity Therapy Group to bring that same standard to more campuses and more families — and to build a team of therapists trained to deliver it consistently.
